# Movie0001 — Editing Bible

## Part I
Favor longer takes during survival and discovery.

Use rapid cutting only when Elias is physically threatened.

## Part II
Cross-cut human and Vein systems as their conflict increases.

## Part III
Editing becomes more associative as Elias's perception changes.

## Continuity
Every transition must preserve:
- spatial geography
- character state
- time
- equipment
- information

## Mystery
Do not cut away merely to hide information. Hide information only when the audience's perspective logically cannot see it.
