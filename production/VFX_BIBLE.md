# Movie0001 — VFX Bible

## VFX-001 — Atmospheric entry
Elias's descent through Nera's atmosphere.

Requirements:
- realistic atmospheric scattering
- debris
- physical motion
- suit interaction
- controlled camera shake

## VFX-002 — Vein response
Subtle energy/information movement through Vein structures.

Avoid electrical-lightning aesthetics.

## VFX-003 — Adaptation
Progressive physiological changes.

Effects must begin almost invisibly and become more apparent across the trilogy.

## VFX-004 — Core network
Large-scale subterranean network visualisation.

The audience must understand structure and scale.

## VFX-005 — Final planetary signal
A subtle network-wide response visible from orbit.

## VFX rule

Every effect must communicate story information.
