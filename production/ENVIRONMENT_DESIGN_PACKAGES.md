# Environment Design Packages

## ENV-001 — Nera Surface
Function: establish planetary hostility, isolation and physical rules.
Design: broad exposed terrain, fractured strata and weather-shaped formations.
Materials: mineral crust, dust, rock and only environmentally justified condensate.
Behaviour: wind, particulate transport, thermal shifts and geological instability.
Story purpose: force Elias underground and establish physical rules.
Forbidden: fantasy landscapes or decorative glowing terrain.

## ENV-002 — Impact Zone
Function: mission failure and survival starting point.
Design: damaged expedition hardware embedded in terrain with believable impact geometry.
Continuity: debris, suit damage, equipment loss and route out remain trackable.
Forbidden: clean or impossible crash debris.

## ENV-003 — Shelter Zone
Function: Elias's first sustainable refuge.
Design: enclosed rock architecture with defensible entrance, resource points, water/chemical cycles and improvised human infrastructure.
Story purpose: survival becomes routine; first Vein evidence appears.
Forbidden: convenient rooms or unexplained technology.

## ENV-004 — Vein Zone
Function: reveal the planetary network.
Design: geology progressively interpenetrated by organic-mineral structures.
Behaviour: structures respond to energy, pressure, chemistry and biological proximity.
Forbidden: roots, cables, neon tubes or giant-computer aesthetics.

## ENV-005 — Core Network
Function: deepest system and Part III climax.
Design: distributed spatial architecture rather than a single chamber.
Scale: humans become reference points inside a larger system.
Behaviour: coordinated energy, matter, information and memory-like patterns.
Forbidden: throne room, humanoid creator or conventional control panel.

## ENV-006 — ORISON Command
Function: institutional counterpoint.
Design: credible deep-space operations architecture; modular, practical and information-dense.
Forbidden: generic villain headquarters.

## ENV-007 — Recovery Craft
Function: retrieval and evacuation.
Design: rugged autonomous deep-space craft with believable propulsion, thermal management, landing/recovery and service access.
Forbidden: decorative spacecraft details with no function.
