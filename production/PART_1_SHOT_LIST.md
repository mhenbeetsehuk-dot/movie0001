## Sequence 03 — Learning to Survive

Focus on observational montage: resource discovery, mapping, failed attempts, correction and routine.

## Sequence 04 — First Sign

Use progressively tighter framing as Elias recognises a repeating pattern.

## Sequence 05 — Adaptation

The first adaptation should be communicated through performance and sound before visual effects.

## Sequence 06 — Underground Reveals Itself

Begin constrained, then reveal enormous subterranean scale.

## Sequence 07 — The Cost

Action should be readable and physically grounded.

## Sequence 08 — Discovery

Use inserts of evidence and Elias's reasoning rather than exposition-heavy dialogue.

## Sequence 09 — Choice

Quiet scene. The decision should feel deliberate.

## Sequence 10 — Ending

A long reveal shot establishes the Core Network without explaining it.
