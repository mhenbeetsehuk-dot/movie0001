Gate 1 establishes the minimum visual and behavioural design system before look development.

## Characters
CH-001 Elias Venn; CH-002 Mara Sloane.

## Environments
ENV-001 Nera Surface; ENV-002 Impact Zone; ENV-003 Shelter Zone; ENV-004 Vein Zone; ENV-005 Core Network; ENV-006 ORISON Command; ENV-007 Recovery Craft.

## Props
PROP-001 Elias expedition suit; PROP-002 Elias communications unit; PROP-003 field tools; PROP-004 route markers; PROP-005 ORISON field kit; PROP-006 extraction system; PROP-007 ancient expedition marker; PROP-008 Vein interface surface.

## VFX
VFX-001 Elias adaptation; VFX-002 Vein response.

## Creature
CRE-001 Nera organisms.

Every approved design must define function, silhouette, material logic, scale, lighting response, wear/damage behaviour, movement/behaviour, story purpose, continuity dependencies and forbidden interpretations.

Gate 1 exits only when principal designs are coherent, major environments have spatial/material rules, essential props have functional designs, Vein behaviour is specified, adaptation stages are distinguishable, creature behaviour is specified and designs can be reproduced consistently.
