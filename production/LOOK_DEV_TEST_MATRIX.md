# Look Development Test Matrix

## LD-01 Elias
Neutral full-body, close face, clean suit, damaged suit, A01 adaptation.
Pass: identity, anatomy, costume continuity and material response.

## LD-02 Nera Surface
Wide landscape, human-scale terrain, atmospheric depth, geology close-up, hazard.
Pass: coherent geology, scale, atmosphere and motivated light.

## LD-03 Shelter
Entrance, sleeping/work area, resource area, equipment integration, first Vein evidence.
Pass: geography reconstructable from shots.

## LD-04 Vein
Isolated structure, active response, human interaction, wide Vein Zone, deep-scale transition.
Pass: organic-mineral identity, functional emission and repeatable response.

## LD-05 Core
Approach, threshold, first wide, Elias scale reference, network response.
Pass: distributed planetary system rather than room/computer.

## LD-06 ORISON
Command space, operator station, recovery preparation, field equipment.
Pass: credible institutional technology.

## LD-07 Recovery Craft
Exterior, interior, landing/recovery system, damaged state.

## LD-08 Adaptation
A01 → A02 → A03 → A04.

## Approval
No production asset is approved from one attractive frame. It must survive multiple views and lighting conditions.
