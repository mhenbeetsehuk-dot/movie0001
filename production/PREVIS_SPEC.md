# Movie0001 — Previs Specification

## Purpose
Test storytelling, camera language, blocking, scale and continuity before final generation.

## Priority sequences
1. The Drop
2. First Shelter
3. Adaptation
4. Underground Reveal
5. Part I Ending

## Required outputs
Rough camera plan, sequence timing, blocking, environment scale, character movement, transitions, VFX placeholders and sound placeholders.

Previs is not final imagery. It exists to solve cinematic problems before expensive production.
