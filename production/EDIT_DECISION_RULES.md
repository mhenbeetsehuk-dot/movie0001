# Editorial Decision Rules

Preserve spatial geography, temporal order, equipment state, injury/fatigue, character knowledge and mystery escalation.

Part I: longer takes for survival/discovery; rapid cutting only for immediate threat.
Part II: cross-cut human systems with Vein responses.
Part III: increasingly associative editing as Elias's perception changes.

Do not cut away solely to hide information the viewpoint should logically reveal. Do not remove necessary setup merely to accelerate runtime. Do not manufacture continuity with arbitrary inserts.
