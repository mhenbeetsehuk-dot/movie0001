# Movie0001 — Dialogue Pass Principles

## Elias
Short, practical statements. He thinks aloud only when solving a problem.

## Mara
Controlled, precise and authoritative. Her dialogue changes as her certainty collapses.

## ORISON personnel
Operational language. Avoid generic villain speeches.

## The Vein
No conventional spoken dialogue.

Its communication is represented through environmental response, pattern, sound and memory.

## Rule
If dialogue merely explains an image, remove or replace it with action.
