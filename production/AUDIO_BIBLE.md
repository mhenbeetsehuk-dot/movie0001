# Movie0001 — Audio Bible

## Part I
Sound should emphasise isolation.

- breath
- suit mechanics
- distant subterranean movement
- stone/mineral resonance
- controlled silence

## Part II
Introduce layered Vein tones.

The audience should begin recognising recurring sonic patterns before understanding their meaning.

## Part III
Human and Vein sound languages gradually merge.

## Dialogue
Dialogue should remain economical.

Silence is a dramatic tool.

## Music
Avoid continuous scoring.

Use score for:
- major discoveries
- transformation
- large-scale revelation
- final choice

## Audio continuity
Recurring Vein signals should use consistent motifs throughout the trilogy.
