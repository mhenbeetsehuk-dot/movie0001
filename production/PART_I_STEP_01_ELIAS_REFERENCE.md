# PART I — STEP 01: CANONICAL ELIAS VENN IMAGE

Status: READY FOR FREE IMAGE GENERATION

Part: I only

## Objective

Create the first production-grade canonical image reference for Elias Venn. This image becomes the visual identity anchor for all later Part I shots.

Do not generate shot images yet. Do not generate adaptation states yet. Do not generate Mara yet.

## Canonical identity

- Name: Elias Venn
- Age: 38
- Role: Systems engineer and autonomous-infrastructure specialist
- Build: Lean, functional, believable; not muscular or heroic
- Face: Intelligent, restrained, weathered but not elderly
- Hair: Short, practical, dark
- Facial hair: Light stubble
- Performance: Observant, controlled, contained
- Costume state: E01 intact expedition suit
- Equipment: Credible technical expedition equipment and mission hardware
- Visual language: Grounded cinematic science fiction
- Lighting: Naturalistic cinematic lighting
- Palette: Restrained, industrial, believable

## Generation prompt

Create a production reference sheet for Elias Venn, a 38-year-old male systems engineer and autonomous-infrastructure specialist stranded during a deep-space expedition.

He has a lean functional build, realistic human proportions, a weathered but intelligent face, short practical dark hair and light stubble. He is not muscular, glamorous, youthful-looking or heroic. His expression is restrained, observant and analytical.

He wears a credible industrial deep-space expedition suit: functional, worn but intact, with realistic life-support components, technical fasteners, utility equipment and mission hardware. The suit must look engineered for a real expedition rather than a superhero costume or fantasy space suit.

Create a clean cinematic character reference sheet showing:
1. full-body front view
2. full-body side view
3. full-body rear view
4. close facial portrait
5. equipment/detail view

Neutral controlled studio/background environment. Consistent anatomy and face across all views. Realistic materials, subtle wear, natural skin texture, physically plausible lighting, restrained cinematic science-fiction aesthetic, high detail, photorealistic film concept art.

The purpose is character continuity for a serious science-fiction feature film. Prioritize identity consistency and believable production design over spectacle.

## Negative prompt

No superhero physique, no bodybuilder proportions, no young model appearance, no military stereotype, no fantasy armor, no glowing suit, no neon circuitry, no excessive holograms, no cyberpunk styling, no anime, no cartoon, no fantasy costume, no exaggerated facial features, no beard growth beyond light stubble, no age drift, no duplicate faces, no extra limbs, no distorted hands, no text labels inside the image, no weapons unless naturally part of mission equipment.

## Free-generation procedure

1. Use a free image generator.
2. Generate several candidates using the exact prompt above.
3. Select the strongest candidate for identity consistency, not the prettiest image.
4. Do not modify the face between candidate generations.
5. Preserve the original generated file.
6. Record the generator, date, model if shown, prompt version, and generation/reference ID if available.
7. Bring the selected image back into the project workflow as REF-C-001.
8. Do not approve it as canon until continuity QA passes.

## Acceptance criteria

The image passes Step 01 only if:

- Elias reads clearly as approximately 38.
- Face is distinctive enough to recognize repeatedly.
- Lean functional build is maintained.
- Suit is credible and non-fantastical.
- Character looks like an engineer, not a soldier or superhero.
- No obvious anatomy defects.
- Front/side/rear views remain recognizably the same person.
- Image has no unwanted text or logos.
- Visual language is compatible with the existing Part I production bibles.
