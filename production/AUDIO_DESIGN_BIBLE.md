# Audio Design Bible

## Nera
Wind, pressure, distant geology, material movement and biological activity.

## Shelter
Intimate sparse sound: breathing, equipment, fabric, tools and water.

## Vein
Vibration, resonance, low-frequency movement, material tone and spatial pattern. Never conventional speech.

## ORISON
Machinery, communications, systems and operational rhythm.

## Music
Support emotional/thematic progression rather than explain the mystery. Avoid generic alien clichés.

Every major visual response receives an intentional audio counterpart or intentional silence.
