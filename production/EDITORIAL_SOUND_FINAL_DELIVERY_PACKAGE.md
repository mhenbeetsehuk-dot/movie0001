# Movie0001 — Editorial, Sound & Final Delivery Package 1.0

## Editorial
Assembly follows locked shot IDs and shooting-draft sequence order. Editorial may trim duration but may not silently change canon, reveal order or continuity.

## Sound
- Human survival: restrained environmental realism.
- Vein: low-frequency geological/biological resonance, patterned responses, pressure/vibration and material sound.
- No conventional Vein dialogue.
- Dialogue remains intelligible and economical.
- Score supports progression from survival → mystery → transformation → choice.

## VFX finishing
Resolve adaptation, Vein response, atmospheric effects, extraction damage, network stabilisation and orbital signal only after picture timing is stable.

## Colour/look
Match approved look-development references. Do not introduce a new palette or material language during finishing without change control.

## Final delivery
Picture lock → VFX lock → sound lock → captions/subtitles → titles/credits → master QC → archive package → GitHub production record.

## Archive
Preserve final masters, project files, approved references, shot metadata, QA logs, continuity records and release version manifest.
