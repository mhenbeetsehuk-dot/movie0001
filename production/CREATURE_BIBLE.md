# Movie0001 — Creature Bible

## Design philosophy

Nera's organisms evolved around the Vein.

They should not resemble familiar Earth animals with cosmetic changes.

## CRE-001 — Vein Grazer

Small-to-medium organism that feeds on mineral/biological growth.

Function:
- establishes ecosystem
- provides Elias with early evidence that the Vein supports life
- should not initially be a threat

## CRE-002 — Defensive Organism

Large organism triggered by disruption of Vein structures.

It is dangerous because it protects territory rather than hunting humans.

## CRE-003 — Deep Organism

Rare organism found near the Core.

It should appear partially integrated with the Vein.

Its behaviour foreshadows Elias's eventual adaptation.

## Creature rules

- Behaviour must follow ecological logic.
- Creatures should not appear simply because a scene needs action.
- Their relationship to the Vein should be visually understandable.
