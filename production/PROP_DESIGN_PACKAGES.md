# Prop Design Packages

## PROP-001 — Elias Expedition Suit
Survival and environmental protection. Layered pressure/thermal/protective system with visible service points and replaceable components.
Damage progression: clean → damaged → field-repaired → heavily degraded.
Forbidden: superhero armour.

## PROP-002 — Communications Unit
Mission telemetry, diagnostics and failed communication. Compact rugged device with physical controls and restrained display.
Forbidden: holographic interface.

## PROP-003 — Field Tools
Repair, sampling, cutting, measurement and shelter construction. Tools solve established problems and cannot become unexplained plot devices.

## PROP-004 — Route Markers
Physical/chemical/low-energy navigation markers. Placement establishes underground geography.

## PROP-005 — ORISON Field Kit
Modular instrumentation, sampling, medical and environmental equipment. Cleaner and more capable than Elias's degraded equipment.

## PROP-006 — Extraction System
Credible recovery hardware with anchoring, power and transport mechanisms. It creates a real choice rather than magical escape.

## PROP-007 — Ancient Expedition Marker
Aged, degraded and partially integrated with the environment. It reveals information progressively.

## PROP-008 — Vein Interface Surface
Natural structural surface with repeatable response zones. Never a touchscreen-like interface.
