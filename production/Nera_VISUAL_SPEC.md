# Movie0001 — Nera Visual Specification

## Surface
Alien but physically plausible.

The surface should avoid generic fantasy landscapes.

### Required qualities
- Geological scale
- Harsh atmospheric conditions
- Sparse adapted life
- Strong evidence of environmental pressure
- Realistic physical materials

## Shelter Zone
Dark, claustrophobic, damp and initially readable as natural geology.

Human survival traces gradually make the space feel inhabited.

## Vein Zone
Organic-mineral structures.

The forms should resemble neither ordinary roots nor exposed computer circuitry exactly.

The visual language combines:
- branching biological forms
- mineral growth
- translucent structures
- internal energy movement

## Core Network
Planetary scale.

The audience should understand that the underground is a network rather than a collection of caves.

## Lighting principle
Light should appear to have a source.

No arbitrary glowing objects merely for aesthetics.

## Camera principle
Part I favours constrained framing.

Part II progressively opens the frame.

Part III allows enormous spatial compositions.
