# Screenplay Lock Protocol

The current Part I–III screenplays are structural first passes, not final shooting drafts.

## Required passes
1. Canon
2. Sequence/scene numbering
3. Character continuity
4. Dialogue economy
5. Visual storytelling
6. Foreshadowing
7. Runtime estimate
8. Shot dependency
9. Final screenplay review

Elias: short, practical, diagnostic.
Mara: controlled, precise, authoritative.
ORISON: operational.
Vein: no conventional spoken dialogue.

Every major reveal requires earlier evidence. No reveal may depend entirely on information introduced at the revelation.

Shooting-draft lock occurs only after all continuity and foreshadowing dependencies are recorded.
