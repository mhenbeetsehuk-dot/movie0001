# Part I Shot Specification Master

Status: PREVIS/DRAFT. Final durations remain editorial decisions.

## S01 — The Drop
Mission failure, hostile environment and survival imperative. Coverage: descent/impact, Elias disorientation, damaged equipment, first environmental threat and movement toward shelter. Continuity: suit E01→E02, injury state and equipment loss.

## S02 — First Shelter
Establish underground survival. Cover entrance geography, shelter selection, resources, improvised systems and first night. Maintain shelter map and equipment inventory.

## S03 — Learning to Survive
Show competence through action: food/water, repairs, measurements, route marking and failed communication. Supplies and repairs must track.

## S04 — First Sign
First evidence of the Vein through a restrained environmental anomaly. Do not explain it fully.

## S05 — Adaptation
First biological consequence. Show physiological irregularity and repeatable correlation with Vein response. A01 only.

## S06 — Underground Reveals Itself
Expand scale through connected geography and a major Vein spatial reveal.

## S07 — The Cost
Resource pressure, injury/fatigue, environmental danger and adaptation consequence. No unexplained recovery.

## S08 — Discovery
Connect the signal to the system through an evidence chain. Earlier clues must matter.

## S09 — Choice
Elias chooses understanding over immediate escape, motivated by accumulated evidence.

## S10 — Ending
Elias descends into the deeper network. This is the threshold into Part II.

Every final shot receives a unique ID, references, asset dependencies, continuity state and QA result before editorial use.
