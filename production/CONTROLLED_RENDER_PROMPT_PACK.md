# Controlled Render Prompt Pack — Next Media Pass 1.0

These are production prompts/constraints for the next individual reference-render session. They are subordinate to the canon and design system.

## Character references

### REF-C-001 — Elias Venn
38-year-old male systems engineer, weathered but not heroic, practical expression, short dark hair, stubble, grounded human proportions, industrial deep-space survival suit, credible mission equipment, restrained palette, cinematic natural lighting, neutral studio background, front/side/back plus face and gear details. No superhero styling.

### REF-C-002 — Mara Sloane
46-year-old female ORISON mission director, intelligent controlled expression, mature field-command presence, practical dark mission suit, credible ORISON equipment, restrained professional styling, front/side/back plus face and gear details. She is a mission director, not a generic scientist or young biologist.

## Environment references

### REF-E-001 — Nera Surface
Dark, hostile and fundamentally uninhabitable alien planetary landscape; severe environmental conditions, low or unsafe human habitability, heavy atmosphere/haze, weak or filtered sunlight, rugged mountains, deep valleys, rivers or chemically altered water systems, sparse hostile vegetation and exposed geology; realistic geology; no fantasy architecture; no monumental alien city; Vein network remains subterranean. Nera should feel dangerous, oppressive and biologically/geologically hostile rather than Earth-like and welcoming.

### REF-E-002 — Impact Zone
Recent capsule crash in harsh Nera terrain; damaged descent capsule, smoke, fire remnants, debris, credible impact geometry; same terrain language as REF-E-001.

### REF-E-003 — Shelter
Underground survival shelter assembled from credible mission hardware and local rock; cramped, functional, repairable; practical lighting; no luxury sci-fi interiors.

### REF-E-004 — Vein Zone
Vast subterranean organic-mineral network embedded in rock; functional blue/teal bioluminescence; energy/chemical/biological signalling visible only through environmental response; no neon tubes, roots, fantasy forest or arbitrary glowing props.

### REF-E-005 — Core Network
Planetary-scale organic-mineral junction; monumental through scale and density rather than architecture; natural rock, mineral, fluid, biological structures and controlled light; no conventional command room; no magical throne/chamber.

### REF-E-006 — ORISON Command
Industrial orbital command environment; mission-specific equipment, restrained interfaces, operational personnel, realistic spacecraft observation geometry; no generic military bridge and no excessive holograms.

### REF-E-007 — Recovery Craft
Credible deep-space recovery vehicle; industrial, modular, functional, physically believable propulsion and access systems; visually related to ORISON Command hardware.
