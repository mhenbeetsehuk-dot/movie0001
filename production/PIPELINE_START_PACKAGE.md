## Approval rule
A reference becomes controlled only when its ID, version, purpose, design-package dependency, continuity implications and approval state are recorded.

## Non-negotiable visual rules
- Nera remains physically credible.
- The Vein is organic-mineral and integrated into geology.
- Bioluminescence is functional, not decorative neon.
- Human technology is industrial and credible.
- No unexplained holographic interfaces.
- No generic fantasy scenery.
- No arbitrary glowing objects.
- No conventional spoken language from the Vein.
- Elias adaptation remains progressive and consequential.
- Scale is communicated through geography, bodies, architecture and camera behaviour.

Controlled look development can begin when every principal identity has a design package, reference-manifest entry, controlled specification, continuity notes and acceptance criteria.

Unrestricted shot generation remains blocked until Gate 2 and Gate 3 are separately passed.
