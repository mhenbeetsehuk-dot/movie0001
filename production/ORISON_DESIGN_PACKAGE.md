# ORISON — Design Package

## Visual identity
Advanced but credible private deep-space technology.

## Design principles
Functional, modular, industrial, expensive and maintained.

## Avoid
Generic military science-fiction styling and excessive holographic interfaces.

## Technology groups
- communications
- life support
- exploration
- recovery
- containment
- extraction

## Internal conflict
ORISON should visually communicate institutional sophistication without making every employee visually identical.
