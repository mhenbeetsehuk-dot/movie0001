## Previs acceptance

A priority sequence passes previs only when:
- geography can be understood without explanation;
- character blocking is repeatable;
- camera positions do not contradict later coverage;
- props and injuries match the shooting draft;
- Vein behaviour obeys V01-V06 rules;
- transitions can be cut into the next sequence;
- timing supports the screenplay rather than replacing it.
