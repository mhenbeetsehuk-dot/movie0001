# Movie0001 — Look Development Brief

## Objective
Create a controlled visual identity before full production.

## Test 01 — Elias
Full-body character, face close-up, expedition suit, damaged survival version and adaptation Stage 1.

## Test 02 — Nera Surface
Wide establishing shot, medium terrain, atmospheric condition and material close-up.

## Test 03 — Shelter Zone
Entrance, survival chamber, strange mark and human equipment integrated into environment.

## Test 04 — Vein
Isolated structure, active response, wide Vein Zone and Core Network scale test.

## Test 05 — Adaptation
Stage 1 sensory change, Stage 2 recovery, Stage 3 visible change and Stage 4 interface.

## Approval criteria
Character continuity; environment continuity; material realism; lighting consistency; VFX integration; repeatability.
