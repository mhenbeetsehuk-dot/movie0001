# Design System

## Visual philosophy
The film should feel discovered rather than decorated. Every visual element must answer: what does it do, why does it exist, what does it tell us, or what changes because of it?

## Materials
Nera geology: dense, fractured, layered and mineral-rich.
Vein: organic-mineral composite, grown/deposited and structurally integrated with geology; emission always has a functional cause.
Human equipment: industrial composites, metals, ceramics, fabrics and transparent protective materials; damage accumulates.

## Lighting
Motivated by star/sun illumination, atmospheric scattering, equipment, Vein emission, reflected geology and thermal/chemical phenomena. No arbitrary cinematic glow.

## Scale
Repeated human-scale references establish the progression from surface to shelter to Vein Zone to Core.

## Camera
Part I stays close to Elias and constrained spaces. Part II progressively reveals environmental scale. Part III becomes spatially ambitious while retaining the human viewpoint.

## Adaptation
A01 subtle physiological irregularity.
A02 detectable repeatable correlation with Vein response.
A03 integrated effect on perception and physical response.
A04 interface capability.

Progression must be gradual and traceable.

## Avoid
Superhero aesthetics, generic military sci-fi, arbitrary cyberpunk neon, fantasy magic effects, clean unexplained technology and glowing objects without function.
