# Movie0001 — Generation Pipeline Specification

## Pipeline
01 STORY LOCK — approved screenplay and continuity.
02 DESIGN LOCK — approved characters, environments, props, creatures and VFX language.
03 LOOK DEV — controlled reference tests.
04 PREVIS — camera, blocking, timing and scene continuity.
05 ASSET BUILD — reusable approved production assets.
06 SHOT GENERATION — individual shots from shot records.
07 EDIT — assemble shots according to approved timing.
08 VFX/COMPOSITING — adaptation, Vein responses and atmosphere.
09 SOUND — dialogue, Foley, ambience, SFX and music.
10 REVIEW — continuity, performance, visuals, audio and story.
11 REVISION — fix rejected shots.
12 MASTER — approved sequence and final project master.

## Versioning
Each asset and shot receives a stable ID and version number.

Example: P1-01-001-v003

## Rejection categories
Story; performance; continuity; visual quality; technical; audio; VFX.

## Golden rule
Never solve a continuity problem by silently changing canon.
