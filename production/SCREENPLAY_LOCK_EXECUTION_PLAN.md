# Movie0001 — Screenplay Lock Execution Plan

## Purpose
Convert the three structural screenplay passes into production-safe shooting drafts without changing established trilogy canon.

## Required passes
1. Canon
2. Scene and sequence numbering
3. Character continuity
4. Dialogue
5. Visual storytelling
6. Foreshadowing
7. Runtime
8. Shot dependencies
9. Production dependencies
10. Final lock review

## Canon audit questions
For every scene:
- Where is Elias?
- What does he physically have?
- What injuries, fatigue and adaptation state does he have?
- What does he know?
- What does the audience know?
- What does the Vein respond to?
- What changes because of the scene?
- Which later reveal does the scene support?
- Does it contradict an established rule?

## Information-control rule
Every major reveal requires earlier evidence. If evidence is missing, add foreshadowing, alter the reveal, or record an explicit canon decision.

## Production rule
No scene becomes shot-generation input until its purpose, location, characters, continuity state, assets and VFX/audio dependencies are known.
