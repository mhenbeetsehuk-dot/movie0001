# VFX Design Package

## VFX-001 — Elias Adaptation
A01 subtle physiological irregularities.
A02 measurable correlation with Vein response.
A03 altered perception and physical response.
A04 controlled interface capability.

Progression is gradual. Effects originate from established story events. Elias never becomes a conventional glowing superhero.

## VFX-002 — Vein Response
Response channels: light emission, vibration, particulate movement, structural movement, local chemical/biological reaction and information-like spatial pattern.
Every response has a trigger.

## VFX-003 — Environmental Integration
Dust, atmosphere, moisture, debris, heat and interaction must integrate generated elements with environments.

Reject effects that obscure performance, contradict continuity, use generic energy beams, introduce unexplained physics or communicate nothing.
