## Prompt control
Every reference prompt must specify subject ID, camera/view, physical materials, lighting, scale, environment, canon constraints, forbidden elements and continuity state. Generate identity sheets before cinematic hero frames.

## Hard visual constraints
No generic fantasy caves, neon tubes, arbitrary glowing objects, magical teleportation, unexplained holograms or conventional spoken Vein language. Human technology remains industrial and credible. Vein light is functional and materially integrated.

## Acceptance
Pass only when identity, material, scale and continuity remain stable across at least three views and two lighting states. Record version, reviewer, date and rejection reason.
