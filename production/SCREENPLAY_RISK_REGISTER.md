# Movie0001 — Structural Screenplay Risk Register

**Purpose:** Identify issues that must be resolved during shooting-draft lock. This register does not silently rewrite canon.

| ID | Part | Risk | Required resolution | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SCR-001 | II | Mara transmission says ORISON knows Elias survived, but timing/source is not yet defined | Establish when the transmission was recorded and how it was preserved | HIGH |
| SCR-002 | II | The “second presence” is described as an artificial intrusion without enough early evidence | Add visual/behavioural evidence before naming it as artificial | HIGH |
| SCR-003 | II | Earlier expedition discovery needs a concrete chronology | Establish expedition date, purpose, failure and relationship to ORISON records | HIGH |
| SCR-004 | II | Elias's altered perception is a major capability jump | Seed A01–A03 evidence in Part I and early Part II | HIGH |
| SCR-005 | II | “Memories that are not his own” could be interpreted as supernatural exposition | Define sensory mechanism through Vein information transfer | MEDIUM |
| SCR-006 | II | Compatibility-test reveal requires earlier evidence | Map signal behaviour, Elias selection and neurological profile across Parts I–II | HIGH |
| SCR-007 | II | Part II ending says Elias disappears “into the light” | Preserve physical geography; Core entry must remain a spatial transition, not magical teleportation | HIGH |
| SCR-008 | III | Ancient intelligence is introduced without sufficient evidence trail | Foreshadow through expedition records, markers and Vein structures | HIGH |
| SCR-009 | III | “Different intelligent species” risks over-expanding the mythology | Establish only what is necessary to explain accumulated network intelligence | MEDIUM |
| SCR-010 | III | ORISON war requires operational geography | Define where extraction, containment and Core access physically occur | HIGH |
| SCR-011 | III | Elias final interface must preserve identity | Define observable limits and costs; avoid generic omniscience | HIGH |
| SCR-012 | III | Final orbital signal needs technical plausibility | Define receiver, detection conditions and signal characteristics before final shooting draft | HIGH |
| SCR-013 | I–III | Adaptation progression must remain continuous | Maintain A01–A04 state ledger across every relevant scene | HIGH |
| SCR-014 | I–III | Vein communication can accidentally become dialogue | Keep communication environmental, patterned, biological or sensory | HIGH |

## Lock principle
These are production risks, not permission to invent new mythology. Resolve each against the existing canon hierarchy.

## Exit condition
No HIGH item remains unresolved when the corresponding screenplay enters final shooting-draft lock.
