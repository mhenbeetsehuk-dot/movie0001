# Movie0001 — Production Bible

**Status:** Pre-pipeline

## Format
Feature-length science-fiction trilogy.

## Locations
Nera surface; impact zone; Shelter Zone; Vein Zone; Core Network; ORISON command environment; ORISON recovery craft.

## Principal assets
Elias, Mara, ORISON equipment, Elias survival equipment, Vein structures, Nera organisms, recovery craft and underground environments.

## Visual priorities
1. Character realism
2. Environmental scale
3. Underground atmosphere
4. Vein biological/mineral structures
5. Consistent visual progression through adaptation

## Sound priorities
Sparse opening soundscape; environmental pressure; subterranean resonance; Vein tonal language; human technology sounds; deliberate silence.

## VFX priorities
**Tier 1:** planet, underground environments, Vein structures, adaptation effects, major creature/environment interactions.
**Tier 2:** ORISON technology, atmospheric effects, background organisms, interface effects.
**Tier 3:** minor environmental detail.

## Production rule
Do not begin expensive asset production until the corresponding story element is locked and continuity checked.
