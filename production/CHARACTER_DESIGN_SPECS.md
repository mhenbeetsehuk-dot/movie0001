# Movie0001 — Character Design Specifications

## CH-001 — ELIAS VENN

### Age
38.

### Physical direction
Lean, functional build. Not exaggeratedly muscular. A working engineer who becomes physically conditioned through survival.

### Face
Weathered, intelligent, restrained expression. Facial design must support subtle performance rather than action-hero exaggeration.

### Hair
Short, practical.

### Costume progression
**Arrival:** technical expedition suit with damaged life-support components.

**Early survival:** repaired expedition clothing.

**Middle:** layered survival clothing, improvised protection.

**Late:** integrated human/planetary visual language without becoming a superhero costume.

### Adaptation progression
Stage 1 — heightened perception.

Stage 2 — physiological recovery and environmental tolerance.

Stage 3 — visible but restrained changes.

Stage 4 — stable interface.

The adaptation must remain biological and believable within the film's science-fiction rules.

## CH-002 — DR. MARA SLOANE

Age 46.

Controlled posture, practical command clothing, minimal ornamentation.

Her design should communicate authority without militarising her unnecessarily.

### Costume progression
Command environment → field expedition gear → damaged field gear during final crisis.

## Performance principle

The central performances should favour restraint, observation and accumulated emotional pressure.
