# Creature Design Package — CRE-001

Native life exists to make Nera feel ecologically functional, not to provide monster encounters.

## Functional categories
1. Micro-organisms supporting chemical cycles.
2. Small mobile organisms feeding on mineral/biological gradients.
3. Larger organisms whose behaviour reveals environmental conditions.

## Behaviour
Organisms react to pressure, chemistry, heat, vibration and available energy. They do not behave like Earth predators without an established evolutionary reason. Their behaviour can foreshadow changes in the Vein.

## Visual rules
Anatomy follows locomotion and environmental requirements. Surface texture relates to habitat. Bioluminescence, if present, has a biological function. No decorative glowing eyes or random neon anatomy.

## Approval test
Reject any creature whose function cannot be explained without saying “because it looks alien.”
