# Movie0001 — Cinematography Bible

## Part I
Camera language is intimate and constrained.

Use:
- close observation
- limited visibility
- motivated handheld movement
- negative space
- long holds during discovery

The audience should feel trapped with Elias.

## Part II
The camera progressively opens.

Human groups and underground scale become more visible.

## Part III
The camera can become expansive.

Planetary-scale compositions are permitted only after the audience has learned the visual grammar.

## Lens principle
Lens choice must support story purpose rather than visual fashion.

## Movement principle
Camera movement should have motivation:
- character movement
- environmental threat
- discovery
- scale reveal
- emotional transition.
