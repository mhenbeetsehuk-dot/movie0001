# Movie0001 — Three-Part Architecture

**Status:** Working structure  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Date:** 2026-10-07

## Part I — THE DROP

### Function
Establish the protagonist, planetary isolation, underground survival and the first major mystery.

### Core movement
Arrival → survival → adaptation → discovery.

### Ending requirement
Part I should end with a discovery or revelation that fundamentally changes the meaning of the protagonist's situation.

---

## Part II — THE ADAPTATION

### Function
Expand the world and reveal that the protagonist's survival and transformation are connected to a larger system.

### Core movement
Understanding → transformation → conflict → revelation.

### Existing development principle
The protagonist has been living underground and adapting to the planet. Part II should build on that history rather than restart it.

### Ending requirement
Part II should expose the deeper truth sufficiently to make the final confrontation unavoidable.

---

## Part III — THE TRUTH

### Function
Resolve the trilogy's central mystery and the protagonist's transformation.

### Core movement
Truth → choice → confrontation → consequence → resolution.

### Ending requirement
The conclusion must resolve the protagonist's central identity/survival conflict and give the trilogy's earlier mysteries a coherent meaning.

---

## Trilogy Spine

**Part I:** A man fights to survive an impossible world.

**Part II:** He discovers that survival has changed him and that his presence on the planet may have a greater purpose.

**Part III:** He discovers the truth and must decide what to do with what he has become.

## Important

The specific causes, antagonists, scientific mechanisms and final explanation remain OPEN until developed and approved.
