# Character Bible

## Elias Venn
Age 38. Systems engineer / autonomous infrastructure specialist. Analytical, resourceful and practical; not an action hero. His advantage is understanding systems under pressure. Weaknesses: isolation, over-reliance on rational control and difficulty accepting non-human intelligence.

Arc: Part I survivor; Part II interface candidate; Part III decision-maker about the relationship between humanity and the Vein.

Dialogue is economical. He speaks when communication, diagnosis or emotional pressure requires it.

Track injuries, hydration, food, fatigue, clothing, equipment, knowledge, emotional state, adaptation stage and location.

## Mara Sloane
Age 46. ORISON mission director. Controlled, intelligent and operationally precise. Not a cartoon villain. She believes dangerous information must be controlled, but her certainty fractures.

Arc: Elias begins as a mission variable; she learns the mission was built around his compatibility; she becomes morally implicated in the abandonment/recovery effort; she ultimately chooses people over ORISON's institutional objective.

## ORISON
Private deep-space systems organization containing competing interests: Retrieval, Containment and Research. Technology is industrial, credible and mission-specific; avoid generic military sci-fi.

## The Vein
Planetary-scale organic-mineral network transferring energy, chemical information, biological signals and encoded patterns. It never uses conventional spoken dialogue. Communication occurs through light, vibration, pressure, biological response, spatial pattern and memory-like information transfer. Its creators are never shown directly.

Every principal character must create a consequence.
