# Movie0001 — Part II Sequence Map
# THE ADAPTATION

01 — THE THRESHOLD: Elias enters the deeper Vein and discovers evidence of an older human expedition.

02 — THE GHOST SIGNAL: A surviving ORISON transmission reaches him. Mara is alive and knows he survived.

03 — THE SECOND PRESENCE: Elias discovers another intelligence interacting with the Vein.

04 — THE RETURN ATTEMPT: ORISON launches a recovery mission.

05 — CONTACT: The recovery team enters Nera and triggers defensive responses.

06 — ELIAS THE INTERFACE: Elias can perceive patterns the team cannot. His adaptation becomes essential.

07 — THE OLD EXPEDITION: Records reveal previous contact and failed attempts to control the Vein.

08 — MARA'S CONFESSION: Mara reveals ORISON selected Elias because his neural profile matched the signal.

09 — THE BREACH: ORISON attempts to extract the Vein's core information.

10 — THE PLANET RESPONDS: The Vein activates across Nera. The underground becomes unstable.

11 — THE CHOICE: Elias chooses between helping ORISON escape with the data or protecting the Vein.

12 — THE REVELATION: The signal is not a call for rescue. It is a compatibility test.

13 — THE SEPARATION: Elias prevents ORISON from taking control, but his adaptation becomes permanent.

14 — END OF PART II: Elias enters the Core Network voluntarily and encounters the source of the signal.
