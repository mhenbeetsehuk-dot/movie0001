## Protagonist

**Name:** Elias Venn  
**Age:** 38  
**Occupation before the incident:** Systems engineer specialising in remote infrastructure and autonomous systems.

Elias is practical, observant and technically capable, but not an action hero. His strongest survival weapon is pattern recognition.

## The Drop

Elias is deliberately dropped onto the planet **Nera**.

He was not sent there as punishment. He was sent because a remote autonomous system detected a signal beneath Nera that matched technology believed to be impossible.

The organization responsible is **ORISON**, a private deep-space systems company operating beyond normal public oversight.

ORISON's stated mission was exploration. Its hidden objective was to determine whether the signal represented an intelligent system.

Elias was selected because he could diagnose complex autonomous systems without relying on a conventional communications network.

## Why He Was Abandoned

The mission was designed around remote extraction.

The extraction system failed.

ORISON classified the mission as unrecoverable and terminated active support.

Elias does not initially know this.

## The Planet

**Name:** Nera.

Nera appears hostile but is not inherently malicious. Its underground environment contains a vast biological-geological network called **the Vein**.

The Vein is not a conventional machine. It is a planetary-scale information and energy network formed through the interaction of living matter, mineral structures and ancient artificial intervention.

## Adaptation

Elias survives because prolonged exposure to the Vein changes his physiology.

The adaptation is gradual.

It initially improves:
- sensory perception
- tolerance of Nera's environment
- recovery from injury
- ability to detect changes in the Vein

It also creates costs:
- altered sleep
- sensory overload
- memory disturbances
- increasing difficulty distinguishing signals from ordinary perception

The adaptation is not a superpower. It is an exchange.

## The Central Truth

The signal that brought ORISON to Nera is not a distress call.

It is a **selection signal**.

The Vein has been searching for a compatible intelligence capable of interfacing with it.

Elias was not randomly selected by ORISON. The signal influenced the mission's selection process.

Whether the Vein deliberately engineered Elias's arrival remains a major mystery until Part III.

## Trilogy Principle

The apparent survival story becomes a story about contact, adaptation and identity.

The final question is not simply whether Elias can escape Nera.

It is whether returning unchanged is still possible—or desirable.
