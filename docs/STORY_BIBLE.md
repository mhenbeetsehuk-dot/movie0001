# Movie0001 — Story Bible

**Status:** Working foundation  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Date:** 2026-10-07

## 1. Core Premise

A man is dropped onto an unknown planet and is forced to survive beneath its surface. Cut off from the world he knew, he adapts to an environment that appears increasingly hostile—and increasingly purposeful.

His struggle for survival gradually becomes a discovery story: the planet, the underground environment, and the changes occurring within him are connected to a larger truth.

**Core dramatic question:** What happens to a human being when survival requires becoming something the world he came from would no longer recognize?

## 2. Established Story Facts

The following are treated as established project facts at this stage:

- A man is dropped onto a planet.
- He survives underground.
- He adapts to the planet's underground environment.
- The story is designed as a three-part narrative.
- The material developed for later parts must remain consistent with the foundational events of Part 1.

## 3. Development Principle

We will distinguish between:

**CANON** — explicitly established facts.

**DEVELOPMENT** — ideas being explored but not yet locked.

**OPEN QUESTION** — information we need to decide before it becomes canon.

No major invention will silently become canon.

## 4. Protagonist

### The Man

**Role:** Primary protagonist.

**Starting condition:** An outsider who has been forcibly placed on an unknown planet.

**Immediate objective:** Survive.

**Long-term dramatic trajectory:** Survival → adaptation → discovery → transformation → confrontation.

**OPEN QUESTIONS**
- Name
- Age
- Profession/background
- Why he was dropped
- Who dropped him
- Whether he was expected to survive
- What equipment he initially possesses
- What he knows about the planet
- What physical/psychological changes occur
- Whether he can return home

## 5. The Planet

The planet is currently an unknown hostile environment.

### Established
- It contains an underground environment in which the protagonist lives and adapts.

### DEVELOPMENT
The planet may contain:
- Unusual biological systems
- Previously unknown forms of life
- Environmental mechanisms that influence adaptation
- Evidence that the protagonist's arrival was not random
- A deeper planetary mystery

These remain possibilities until approved.

## 6. The Underground

The underground is not merely a hiding place. It is the protagonist's primary environment and therefore a major storytelling element.

It should evolve through the trilogy:

**Part 1:** Threatening and unfamiliar.

**Part 2:** Increasingly understood, revealing deeper systems and connections.

**Part 3:** Recontextualized; what appeared to be a prison, refuge, ecosystem, or threat may have a larger purpose.

## 7. Adaptation

Adaptation is a central theme.

The protagonist's adaptation should have consequences. Survival should require choices, sacrifice and change rather than functioning as a simple physical upgrade.

Potential dimensions of adaptation:
- Physical
- Sensory
- Psychological
- Behavioural
- Environmental
- Social
- Biological

These dimensions are DEVELOPMENT until specifically established.

## 8. Central Themes

Current thematic candidates:

- Survival versus identity
- Humanity versus adaptation
- Isolation
- Transformation
- The unknown
- The consequences of forced evolution
- Whether returning to one's old life is possible after fundamental change

## 9. Mystery Architecture

The story should reveal information progressively.

### Part 1
The audience primarily asks:
- Where is he?
- Why was he brought here?
- How can he survive?
- What is beneath the surface?

### Part 2
The questions become:
- What is happening to him?
- What is the true nature of the planet?
- Who or what is connected to his arrival?
- What does his adaptation mean?

### Part 3
The central questions become:
- Why was he really sent there?
- What is the full truth?
- What choice must he make?
- What will his transformation ultimately mean?

These are structural questions, not yet fixed answers.

## 10. Continuity Rule

Any future screenplay scene must be checked against this document.

If a new idea contradicts established canon, the contradiction must be explicitly resolved rather than silently overwritten.
