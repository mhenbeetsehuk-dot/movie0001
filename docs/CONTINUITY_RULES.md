# Movie0001 — Continuity Rules

## Canon hierarchy
1. Core canon
2. Trilogy lock
3. Story bible
4. Character/world bibles
5. Screenplay
6. Shot framework
7. Production assets

Conflicts must be resolved explicitly.

## Elias continuity
Track injuries, equipment, food/water, physical adaptation, knowledge, emotional state and location.

## Mystery continuity
Every major reveal must have earlier evidence. No reveal may depend entirely on information introduced at the moment of revelation.

## Visual continuity
Vein structures, adaptation effects and ORISON technology retain consistent design language across all three parts.
