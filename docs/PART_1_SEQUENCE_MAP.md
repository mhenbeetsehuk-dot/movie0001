# Movie0001 — Part I Sequence Map

**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Structural draft

This document will contain the complete Part I sequence architecture before screenplay drafting.

## Sequence 01 — The Drop
**Purpose:** Establish the protagonist's arrival and immediate isolation.

**Required beats:**
- The protagonist is dropped onto the planet.
- Immediate environmental danger.
- Loss of normal support.
- Forced movement toward survival.

**OPEN:** Exact circumstances and visual execution.

---

## Sequence 02 — First Shelter
**Purpose:** Establish the underground environment.

**Required beats:**
- Discovery of shelter or entry point.
- First signs that the underground is not empty.
- Basic survival problem.
- First night/period of isolation.

---

## Sequence 03 — Learning to Survive
**Purpose:** Move from panic to deliberate survival.

**Required beats:**
- Food/water problem.
- Environmental hazard.
- First successful survival adaptation.
- Discovery of repeatable rules.

---

## Sequence 04 — The First Sign
**Purpose:** Introduce the central mystery.

The protagonist encounters evidence that suggests the planet is more complicated than an untouched hostile world.

---

## Sequence 05 — Adaptation
**Purpose:** Establish that the protagonist himself is changing.

The change should initially be subtle enough that he questions whether it is real.

---

## Sequence 06 — The Underground Reveals Itself
**Purpose:** Expand the world.

The protagonist reaches a previously inaccessible area and discovers evidence that changes his understanding of the underground.

---

## Sequence 07 — The Cost
**Purpose:** Make adaptation consequential.

The protagonist gains a survival advantage but pays a meaningful physical or psychological price.

---

## Sequence 08 — The Discovery
**Purpose:** Deliver the major Part I revelation.

The discovery should reframe the question from:

**"How do I survive here?"**

to:

**"Why am I here?"**

The exact revelation remains OPEN.

---

## Sequence 09 — The Choice
**Purpose:** End Part I with agency.

The protagonist chooses a course of action based on the discovery.

---

## Sequence 10 — Part I Ending
**Purpose:** Create the bridge into Part II.

The ending should establish that the protagonist's situation is part of something larger and make continued exploration unavoidable.

---

## Structural Rule

These sequences are a framework, not final screenplay scenes. Before drafting pages, each sequence will receive:
- Location
- Characters
- Objective
- Conflict
- Turning point
- Reveal
- Emotional beat
- Visual set piece
- Continuity notes
