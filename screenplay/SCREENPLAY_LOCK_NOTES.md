## Canon corrections
- Protagonist: Elias Venn.
- Planet: Nera.
- Organization: ORISON.
- Planetary network: the Vein.
- Elias was deliberately sent to Nera.
- ORISON eventually abandons extraction.
- Elias's adaptation is gradual and consequential.
- The trilogy ends with Elias remaining on Nera and a final signal reaching orbit.
